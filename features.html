How it works

From question paper to result in three steps

  1. Teacher publishes

    Upload questions, set the time, pass mark and monitoring options, then share the assessment code.

  2. Student takes it

    Students log in with their PIN or enter the code, then answer on any phone, tablet or computer.

  3. Results straight away

    Scores, corrections and certificates appear immediately, and teachers see results, positions and progress.

01

Create and publish assessments

Teachers can have an assessment ready for students in minutes.

  • Upload questions from a CSV file or build them from the question bank
  • Add pictures and diagrams to questions; maths is displayed in proper notation
  • Set the time limit, pass mark, shuffling, monitoring and whether students see results
  • Choose roster students only, or anyone with the assessment code
  • Share the assessment code on WhatsApp in one tap
  • Edit settings later and reuse a past assessment from CSV History
02

A focused exam screen for students

Clear on any phone, tablet or computer.

  • Colour-changing circular countdown timer
  • Question number boxes for quick movement between questions
  • Student photo shown beside the name
  • Optional read-aloud for questions and options, with voice choices
  • Answers are saved as the student goes, so a refresh or a weak connection does not wipe their work
  • Automatic submission of saved answers when time runs out
03

Fair assessments with live monitoring

Tools that help teachers keep every attempt honest.

  • Optional camera and noise monitoring, switched on by the teacher per assessment
  • A consent notice students must accept before starting a monitored assessment
  • Live view so a teacher can watch, and listen to, a student taking a monitored assessment
  • Live messages to one student or everyone during an assessment
  • A warning for rushing, and a flag on the teacher’s result
  • Access logs and activity logs for review
04

Results, positions and progress

Marking is automatic, and the numbers are ready straight after submission.

  • Instant scores with full corrections showing right and wrong answers
  • Class positions for each assessment and a whole-class broadsheet across subjects
  • Student progress history over time
  • Filter results, then export to CSV or PDF
  • Re-grade results after correcting an answer key
  • Allow a single student to retake; download a backup of results and questions
05

Certificates of achievement

Presentable certificates without any design work.

  • Certificates carry the student’s photo and the teacher’s saved signature
  • Download as PDF or image
  • Download many certificates at once as a ZIP of separate PDFs
  • School name and logo can appear for schools set up on CleverMent
06

Reports parents can read

Shareable PDF reports built from the assessment results.

  • A report for one assessment, or an overall report across assessments
  • Performance details with automatic written recommendations
  • Space for the teacher’s own note
07

Live quiz competitions

A live, game-style quiz for the classroom or the whole school.

  • Players join with a room code; the host chooses roster students only or open joining
  • Questions from existing assessments or a CSV made for the competition
  • Faster correct answers score more; the quiz moves on by itself with a top-10 leaderboard
  • Sound effects and a get-ready countdown
  • Results are saved and can add to a student’s progress history
08

My Learning Space

Students study and practise on their own, at their own pace.

  • Lessons and practice for Primary 6 Mathematics, English Studies and Basic Science and Technology
  • Each subject is organised by topic, with illustrated lessons and worked examples
  • Practice questions with explanations, in the same look as a real assessment
  • Students choose how many questions to practise
09

Student accounts and messages

Everything a student needs in one place.

  • Roster students log in with a PIN given by their teacher
  • A home page with assessments to do, done, closed, results, progress and certificates
  • Teachers message a whole class, selected students or one student; students can reply
  • Phone alerts for new assessments and messages, for devices that support them
10

Administration and schools

Control for owners, school admins and teachers.

  • Separate dashboards for administrators, teachers and students
  • Schools with their own name, logo, colours and a dedicated school link
  • A school admin manages that school’s teachers
  • Admins can switch individual teacher features on or off
  • Login lockout after repeated wrong admin passwords
11

CleverBot assistant

Built-in help for teachers.

  • Ask general questions, or get help with CleverMent itself
  • Generate questions in the exact CleverMent CSV format, ready to upload

Good to know: camera and noise monitoring, live watching and phone alerts depend on the teacher turning them on and on the student’s device and browser permissions. CleverMent works in a web browser, so there is nothing to install.
