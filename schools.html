Getting started

Start in four steps

  1. Set up

    Teachers can sign up on their own. To use CleverMent as a school with its own name and logo, message us on WhatsApp.

  2. Add your classes

    Create class rosters with student names, admission numbers and photos, and give each student a PIN.

  3. Publish assessments

    Upload questions, choose who may take it, set the time and monitoring, and share the code.

  4. Review results

    See marked results, positions and progress, then share certificates and parent reports.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do students need to install anything?

No. CleverMent runs in a web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Phone alerts are optional and depend on the device and browser.

Can a school have its own name and logo?

Yes. A school set up on CleverMent can have its own name, logo and colours, and a dedicated school link for students. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange this.

Can a teacher use CleverMent without joining a school?

Yes. Teachers can sign up independently, or be added to a school by that school’s admin.

How do students get into an assessment?

Roster students log in with a PIN from their teacher. For open assessments, students enter the assessment code. The teacher chooses which applies when publishing.

Can we stop students from copying each other?

Teachers can shuffle questions, switch on camera and noise monitoring, watch students live and flag very fast answering. These tools discourage cheating but cannot guarantee it.

What do parents receive?

Teachers can create a PDF report for one assessment or an overall report, with performance details, written recommendations and a personal note from the teacher.
